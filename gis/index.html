    Different GIS systems

    GIS may appear as web maps, desktop software, mobile data-collection tools, or data services that power other apps. Each connects information to locations.

    Web GIS

    Runs in a browser, making maps and layers easier to view and share with a team.

    Gallup offers public web maps.

    Desktop GIS

    Runs as installed software and is often used for detailed editing, analysis, and finished maps.

    Best for: advanced map work

    Mobile GIS

    Uses a phone or tablet to collect a location, observation, image, or condition in the field.

    Best for: field data

    GIS data services

    Deliver map layers or location data to websites and applications.

    Best for: connecting tools
